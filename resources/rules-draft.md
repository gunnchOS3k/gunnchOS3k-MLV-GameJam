# Rules Draft

**DRAFT — not final until published before registration**

Planned **Lake County Phone-First Game Jam** · Summer 2026

---

## 1. Eligibility

- Open to participants in and connected to **Northwest Indiana** and virtual attendees who follow community rules  
- Solo or team submissions allowed  
- Minors: **planned** with guardian consent (forms coming)  
- Registration **coming soon**

## 2. Timeline

- Follow **proposed schedule** on the [public site](../docs/index.html#timeline)  
- Submissions must meet deadline (proposed **July 27, 2026**)  
- Theme and constraints apply from **kickoff** onward  

## 3. Theme and constraints

- Official theme revealed at kickoff only  
- Submissions must address theme and published constraints  

## 4. Original work and assets

- You must have rights to all submitted content  
- Credit assets, collaborators, and libraries  
- No copyrighted material without permission  
- AI may be used as a helper with **disclosure**; you must understand your project  

## 5. Submission requirements

See [Submission Guide](submission-guide.md). Minimum: title, description, theme interpretation, how to play, link or files, demo (recommended).

## 6. Phone-first and virtual participation

- Laptop **not required**  
- Friday office hours **optional**  
- Full jam completable online  

## 7. Safety and content

- No harassment, hate, explicit sexual content, or doxxing  
- No private personal data in public builds  
- Family-appropriate content expected  
- Organizers may disqualify unsafe entries  

## 8. Conduct

- Follow community rules published at registration  
- Reference: [MLH Code of Conduct](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md) for respect and inclusion standards  

## 9. Judging

See [Judging and Awards](judging-and-awards.md). Judges’ decisions are final for award categories.

## 10. Prizes

- Awards are **planned**; depend on sponsors  
- No promise that every participant receives cash or prizes  

## 11. Platform

- Submission platform **coming soon** (itch.io, Devpost, or hybrid TBD)
