# FAQ — Lake County Phone-First Game Jam

**Official jam:** [itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam](https://itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam)

---

## Do I need a laptop?

**No.** A phone is enough for many submission types.

---

## Do I need to know how to code?

**No.** Paper prototypes, Slides stories, GDevelop, and demo videos are valid.

---

## Are there prizes?

**No cash prizes** for this launch edition. There is public recognition, bragging rights, showcase visibility, and real **portfolio / proof-of-work** value.

---

## How do I join?

**Join on itch.io:** [lake-county-phone-first-game-jam](https://itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam)

---

## Where do I submit?

On **itch.io** — create a project page under the jam and submit before the deadline.

---

## What is the deadline?

**Saturday, July 11, 2026 at 11:59 PM Central.**

---

## Is Discord required?

Discord is the main **community and help** space once the invite is posted, but official rules, dates, and submissions live on **itch.io** and this GitHub knowledge base.

**Discord channel: coming soon.**

---

## Can I use my phone?

**Yes.** This is a phone-first jam by design.

---

## Can I submit a paper prototype?

**Yes**, if rules, photos, and a demo video make it understandable.

---

## Can I join late?

**Yes**, if submissions are still open on itch.io before the deadline.

---

## Can minors participate?

**Yes**, with appropriate guardian consent and safety expectations. No private one-on-one adult/minor mentoring. See [Parents and Guardians](parents-and-guardians.md).

---

## What if I do not finish?

Submit what you have with a clear demo and reflection. A small finished game beats a giant unfinished one.

---

## What is the theme?

Revealed when the jam opens **Friday, June 5, 2026**. Practice tools early; final submissions must fit the official theme.

---

## Can I use AI?

**Yes, as a helper** — with disclosure and respect for copyright and safety. See [AI Use Policy](ai-use-policy.md).

---

## Do I have to attend Friday office hours?

**No.** The full jam can be completed virtually. See [Office Hours](office-hours.md).

---

## Where will in-person office hours happen?

Optional physical hubs are **candidates only** — not confirmed. Friday sessions are **virtual-first** on Discord when live.
