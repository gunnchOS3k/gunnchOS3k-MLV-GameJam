# Discord Participation Guide

**Official Discord Game Jam channel: coming soon**

The organizer will use Discord for announcements, help, optional Friday office hours, and community check-ins. The invite link will be posted on the [itch.io jam page](https://itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam) and this site when ready.

---

## Before you join

1. Read the [Rules](rules.md) and [Parents guide](parents-and-guardians.md) if you are under 18 or a guardian.  
2. Have your [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam) account ready for official jam info and submissions.  
3. This GitHub site remains the **knowledge base** — Discord is for live community support.

---

## When the invite is posted

1. Join the Discord server using the official invite only.  
2. Find the **Lake County Phone-First Game Jam** category/channels.  
3. Read **#game-jam-rules** first.  
4. Introduce yourself in **#game-jam-intros** safely (see below).  
5. Use **#game-jam-help-desk** for beginner questions.  
6. Use **#game-jam-team-formation** to find collaborators.

---

## Safe introductions

Share only what you are comfortable with publicly:

- Name or handle  
- Interests (games, art, story, code, design)  
- Tool comfort level (beginner, phone-only, etc.)  
- What kind of teammate you are looking for  

**Do not post:** home address, passwords, school ID numbers, phone numbers, medical details, or private family information.

---

## Communication rules

- Be respectful — the community may include minors.  
- Ask questions in **public help channels** when possible.  
- Use **threads** for project-specific questions when available.  
- **No private one-on-one adult/minor mentoring.**  
- Mentors and adults should keep support **visible and group-based**.  
- Report harassment, bullying, or unsafe behavior to moderators/organizers immediately.  
- Do not share copyrighted or explicit content.

## Recommended roles

- Organizer  
- Moderator  
- Judge  
- Mentor  
- Participant  
- Parent/Guardian (optional)  
- Bot  

**Youth safety:** Prefer **not** to display age labels publicly. Safety is based on behavior, moderation, guardian consent, and **no private adult/minor mentoring** — not public minor tags.

---

## Moderator reminders

- Watch for doxxing and private info in screenshots.
