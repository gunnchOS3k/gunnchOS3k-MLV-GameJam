# Community Build Hubs

**Candidate locations only — not confirmed · Not official library partners**

## Purpose

Rotating **Friday office hours** showcase Northwest Indiana spaces where people can learn, build, and collaborate — libraries, community centers, and partner venues with reliable WiFi.

---

## Hub evaluation checklist

Before booking a hub, confirm:

- [ ] Reliable WiFi (ask about speed and capacity)  
- [ ] Enough electrical outlets  
- [ ] Seating for **15–40** people  
- [ ] Public transit or ride accessibility  
- [ ] Youth-safe, welcoming environment  
- [ ] Restrooms available  
- [ ] Physical accessibility (entrances, aisles, restrooms)  
- [ ] Staff or volunteer check-in process  
- [ ] Permission for a group reservation  
- [ ] Policy for minors / guardians if relevant  
- [ ] Friday late afternoon or **evening** availability  
- [ ] Food and drink policy  
- [ ] Public computer access (bonus)  
- [ ] Parking and transit info  
- [ ] Photo/media rules  
- [ ] Insurance or certificate of insurance (COI) requirements
