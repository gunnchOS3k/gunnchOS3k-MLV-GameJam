# Friday Office Hours

**Optional · Not required · Locations pending confirmation**

## Purpose

Friday office hours are **optional** in-person build and help sessions during the jam. They exist to:

- Build camaraderie  
- Offer mentorship in **visible group settings**  
- Provide WiFi and outlet access  
- Help troubleshoot phone-first tools  
- Remind participants that **Northwest Indiana has real build spaces**  

## Important

> **You can complete the entire jam virtually. Office hours are support, not a barrier.**

**Fridays during the jam** — specific times and addresses will be announced when candidate hubs are confirmed.

- Quiet and collaborative zones where possible
