# AI Use Policy

**Lake County Phone-First Game Jam**

AI is **allowed as a helper** — not as a substitute for understanding your own project.

---

## Allowed uses

- Brainstorming game ideas, stories, and mechanics  
- Explaining tools or code in plain language  
- Drafting README text, rules, or tutorial copy you then review and edit  
- Balancing simple game values in Sheets  
- Outlining pitch decks or demo scripts  

---

## Requirements

1. **Disclose** meaningful AI use on your itch.io page and in your Proof of Work Packet.  
2. **Understand** what you submit — you should be able to explain your game in your own words.  
3. **Do not** claim work as fully human-created if AI had a major role without disclosure.  
4. **Do not** use AI to generate harassment, explicit content, hate material, or **copyrighted** content you do not have rights to.  
5. **Credit** AI tools alongside other tools and tutorials (e.g., “Story outline drafted with Gemini, edited by team”).  

---

## Not allowed

- Submitting AI output you cannot explain to judges  
- Using AI to imitate copyrighted characters, music, or art without permission  
- Generating unsafe or inappropriate content for a youth-inclusive community jam  

---

## For parents and educators

AI is a learning assistant here, not a way to skip learning. Encourage participants to edit, test, and own their final project.
