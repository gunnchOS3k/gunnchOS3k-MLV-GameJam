# Asset Licensing Guide

Use assets you **created**, have **permission** to use, or are **properly licensed**.

---

## Safe sources

- Your own art, music, code, and writing  
- Assets you commissioned with clear permission  
- **Public domain** or **Creative Commons** assets that allow your use (check license type: CC0, CC-BY, etc.)  
- Built-in assets from tools (GDevelop, Canva templates, etc.) — follow each tool’s license  
- Free/license-clear resources listed by the tool (read terms each time)  

---

## Risky — avoid without permission

- Commercial game sprites, music, or fonts ripped from other games  
- Disney, anime, sports team, or brand logos you do not own  
- Songs from streaming services (usually not licensed for your game)  
- Random Google Image results without license verification  

---

## Credit everything

On your itch.io page and README, credit:

- Collaborators by name or handle (with permission)  
- Asset packs and artists  
- Code tutorials or snippets  
- AI tools if they helped significantly  
- Fonts, sound effects, and music sources  

---

## AI-generated assets

If AI helped create art, music, or text:

- Disclose it (see [AI Use Policy](ai-use-policy.md))  
- Confirm your tool’s terms allow use in a public game jam submission  
- Do not use AI to recreate copyrighted characters or songs
