# Accessibility and Inclusion

Everyone belongs in this jam — including phone-only participants, beginners, and people who have never made a game before.

## Design goals

- **Phone-first** paths for building and submitting  
- **Browser-playable** games encouraged where possible  
- **Written instructions** required (plain language)  
- **Simple controls** encouraged (one button, taps, keys)  
- **Low storage** path via Bare Bones Toolkit  
- **Virtual participation** — no travel required  
- **Optional** Friday office hours  

## Encouraged in submissions

- Text instructions or in-game tutorial  
- Captions or text equivalent for important audio  
- Color choices that work for common color vision needs  
- Adjustable or forgiving difficulty where possible  
- Demo video with clear narration or on-screen text  

## Inclusion

- Respect all backgrounds, experience levels, and identities  
- No harassment, bullying, or hate content  
- Team names and game content appropriate for a youth-inclusive community event  

## AI use

AI may be used as a **helper** with **disclosure**. Participants must understand their project and follow copyright rules.

## Assets

Use assets you created, have permission to use, or that are properly licensed. **Credit** collaborators and sources.

## Need accommodations?

Contact organizers via Discord help desk (when live) or the itch.io jam page. We will document how to request support (captioning, timing extensions where fair, etc.).
