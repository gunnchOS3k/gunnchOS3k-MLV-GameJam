# Rules — Lake County Phone-First Game Jam

**Official jam page:** [itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam](https://itch.io/jam/lake-county-phone-first-game-jam)

These rules mirror the live itch.io page and add Discord/community guidance for this knowledge base.

---

## Theme

The official theme will be revealed when the jam begins on **Friday, June 5, 2026**. The theme will be broad enough for beginners, phone-only participants, artists, writers, designers, storytellers, and experienced developers.

---

## Core rules (from itch.io)

1. Your submission must respond to the **official theme**.
2. **Beginners are welcome.** No coding experience is required.
3. **Phone-only submissions are valid.**
4. Solo and team submissions are allowed.
5. Games may be digital, physical, paper-based, story-based, video-demonstrated, or prototype-based.
6. Submissions must be appropriate for a community event that may include minors.
7. No harassment, bullying, hate content, doxxing, explicit sexual content, or unsafe content.
8. Do not publish private personal information.
9. Use assets you created, have permission to use, or are legally allowed to use.
10. Credit collaborators, asset sources, tools, and AI assistance.
11. The host may remove submissions that violate safety, conduct, or copyright expectations.

---

## Constraints (participation expectations)

- Respond to the official theme once revealed.
- Build something **playable, watchable, readable, or explainable**.
- Include a **phone-friendly path** where possible.
- Submit enough explanation for judges to understand your project (Proof of Work Packet encouraged).
- Keep projects appropriate for a community event that may include minors.
- Credit assets, collaborators, tools, tutorials, and AI assistance.

See [AI Use Policy](ai-use-policy.md) and [Asset Licensing](asset-licensing.md).

---

## Discord and community conduct

- Join the Discord once the invite is posted (channel coming soon).
- Read rules before posting.
- Ask for help in **public** channels — not private DMs with adults for minors.
- **No private one-on-one adult/minor mentoring.**
- Do not post home addresses, passwords, school IDs, phone numbers, or sensitive personal data.
- Report concerns to moderators/organizers.

See [Discord Guide](discord-guide.md).

---

## Prizes

**No cash prizes** for this launch edition. Recognition is for bragging rights, public showcase, learning, and portfolio proof of work.
